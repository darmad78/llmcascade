# Licensing FAQ (plain English)

This is a short summary only. The full terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE). That file is a **draft**, not legal advice — have a lawyer review it before relying on the royalty / commercial-use clause.

## Can I use this for free?

**Yes**, for personal, educational, and **internal non-revenue** use (including self-hosting for yourself or your company when that use does not charge customers or generate revenue from the software).

## Can I sell a product built on this?

**Not under this license alone.** Selling a product, charging for access, running a paid hosted/SaaS offering, or otherwise generating revenue with llmrouter (or a modified version) requires a separate paid **Commercial License** and royalty terms agreed with the Licensor **before** that use starts.

Contact: **support@conceptgame.co.uk**

## What if I just self-host it for my own company internally?

**Usually free**, if it is internal and **not** part of a revenue-generating product or service you charge for. If llmrouter powers something you bill customers for (including a managed/hosted offering), you need a Commercial License.

## When does it become fully open source?

On the **Change Date (10 August 2030)**, the Licensed Work automatically becomes available under the **Apache License, Version 2.0** instead of this source-available license.
