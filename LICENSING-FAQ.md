# Licensing FAQ (plain English)

This is a short summary only. The full terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE) (**llmrouter Source-Available License v1.1**). That file is a **draft**, not legal advice — have a lawyer review it before relying on the commercial-use, royalty, remedy, or governing-law terms.

## Can I use this for free?

**Yes**, for **Non-Commercial Use**: personal projects, education, academic research, and internal organizational use where nothing built on llmrouter is sold, monetized, or offered to external customers / the public for payment.

## Can I sell a product built on this?

**Not under this license alone.** That is **Commercial Use** and requires a separate written **Commercial License** (with royalty/fee terms) agreed with the Licensor **before** such use starts.

Contact: **support@conceptgame.co.uk**

## What if I just self-host it for my own company internally?

**Usually free**, if it stays **Non-Commercial Use** (internal only; not sold or offered to customers for payment). If llmrouter powers a product/service you charge for — including a hosted/managed offering for others — you need a Commercial License.

## When does it become fully open source?

On the **Change Date (10 August 2030)**, Section 3 (Commercial Use restriction) terminates and the Licensed Work becomes available under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**.
