Goal: find whether anyone **paid** for 429 / multi-provider routing. Not whether they like the tool.

## DM template

> Hi — I'm building a self-hosted BYOK router for free-tier 429s (llmcascade). I'm not selling yet; trying to learn whether people actually pay to solve this. Would you spare 15 minutes for a quick call about how you handle rate limits today? Happy to share my setup notes either way.

## Call script (15 min)

1. What are you building? (agent, app, internal tool)
2. Walk me through the last time you hit a **429** — what broke?
3. What did you do? (wait, rotate keys, LiteLLM, OpenRouter, custom script, paid tier)
4. **Have you or your company spent money** to fix this? (hosting, OpenRouter credits, paid API, contractor time billed)
5. If you could wave a wand, what would you pay for — reliability, cost, keys on-host, observability?
6. Would £9/month for multi-worker budget sync + catalog updates be interesting, or is $0 OSS enough?
